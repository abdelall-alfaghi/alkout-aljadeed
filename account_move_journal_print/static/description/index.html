The situation

Odoo prints invoices, bills and payment receipts. A miscellaneous entry, an accrual or a year-end provision has no PDF at all, so when your auditor or manager wants a signed voucher, someone ends up copying the lines into a spreadsheet.

This module adds a Journal Entry report to the Print menu of every entry. It shows exactly what was booked, tells you whether debit and credit match, and leaves room for the people who check and approve it to sign.

What it adds

Print › Journal Entry
A report on every entry
Available on the entry form and on every journal entry, invoice and bill list. The PDF is named after the entry, so MISC/2026/09/0003 saves as Journal Entry - MISC_2026_09_0003.pdf.
Without it: no PDF for entries that aren't invoices or payments.
account / partner / label / analytic
The lines as booked
Account code and name, partner, label, debit and credit in company currency. The analytic split prints with account names and percentages, one per line, so a 25/75 cost split is visible on paper.
Amount in Currency
Foreign currency column
Appears only when a line is in another currency than the company's, next to the debit and credit it was converted to. Entries in your own currency keep a narrow table.
✓ Balanced
Balance check
A bold total row, then a line that says Balanced, or Unbalanced with the exact difference. Useful when you print drafts for review before posting.
DRAFT watermark
Status you can't miss
Posted entries get a green label. Drafts and cancelled entries get a red label and a large diagonal watermark, so nobody files a draft as final.
Prepared · Checked · Approved
Signature boxes
Prepared by is filled with the person who created the entry and when. Checked by and Approved by are left empty for handwritten signatures. A Printed by line records who printed it and at what time.
Journal Entry (Partner Language)
Print in the partner's language
A second report prints labels, dates and amounts in the partner's language, and falls back to yours when the entry has no partner.
list › select › Print
Mass printing
Select entries in any list and print once: one PDF, one entry per page.

Getting started

Install

Open Apps, clear the Apps filter, search for Print Journal Entries and click Activate. It only needs Invoicing, which every accounting database already has.

Apps › account_move_journal_print › Activate
Use

Open a journal entry, or tick several in Accounting › Journal Entries, then choose Print › Journal Entry. There is nothing to set up. The report uses your company's document layout.

Tick several entries in the list and print them as one PDF, one entry per page.
A draft entry prints with a red label and a diagonal DRAFT watermark.

Fine print

Which entries can I print?
Every journal entry: miscellaneous entries, invoices, bills, payments and bank entries. Section and note lines are left out of the table.
Who can print it?
Anyone who can read the entry. The report adds no new access rights.
Does it change how entries are posted?
No. It only reads the entry to print it. Nothing is written to your journals, and uninstalling it removes the two reports and nothing else.
Where can I run it?
On Odoo.sh and on your own server, Community or Enterprise, versions 16.0 to 20.0. Odoo Online doesn't accept third-party modules.
What doesn't it do?
It doesn't add an approval workflow or record who posted the entry; the signature boxes are for paper. Columns and layout are fixed, and there are no subtotals per currency.
